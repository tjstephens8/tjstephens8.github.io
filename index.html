Permitting & inspections

Digital development-services workflows

I mapped existing processes, configured permit and inspection workflows, built mobile forms and reports, tested the system, trained users, and supported the rollout.

Public safety

A permanent geographic framework for measuring response.

To expand the fire department's accounting zones and support response-time analysis, I rebuilt the system on standard quarter-section lines. The result is a permanent reference grid that assigns each call to a consistent location—even beyond the department's normal service area—while remaining stable as streets, development, and boundaries change.

The shared zone system supports clearer reporting now and creates a reliable foundation for comparing response patterns over time.
