What I do

More than maps.
Operational systems.

As the sole GIS professional for the City of Scottsbluff, I connect people, data, and software across departments with very different needs.

My work sits at the intersection of enterprise GIS, product ownership, business analysis, and implementation. I translate complicated processes into dependable tools—and stay close enough to the users to know whether those tools are working.

How I work

Practical delivery from ambiguity to adoption.

The technology matters. The process—and the trust around it—matters more.

01

Listen before building

Start with the people doing the work, the outcome they need, and the constraints they cannot ignore.

02

Translate the workflow

Turn departmental language and manual steps into clear requirements, data structures, and usable interfaces.

03

Test in the real world

Put tools in front of actual users, learn where they break down, and adjust before a broad rollout.

04

Own the outcome

Train, document, support, measure, and keep improving after launch. Deployment is the beginning, not the finish line.
