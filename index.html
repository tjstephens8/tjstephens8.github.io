03 / Operational GIS

Public safety

A permanent geographic framework for measuring response.

The fire department needed to expand its accounting zones so response times could be calculated consistently across a much larger service area. I rebuilt the system around standard quarter-section lines, creating stable zones that do not depend on changing streets, jurisdictions, or development patterns.

01 / Expand

Extended the accounting-zone structure beyond the existing service area so calls could be assigned to consistent geographic units wherever a response occurs.

02 / Measure

Connected incident locations to permanent quarter-section identifiers, supporting repeatable response-time reporting and future analysis.

Why quarter sections?

They provide a standardized, durable reference grid that remains useful as service boundaries change and gives the department a framework for calls beyond its normal coverage area.
